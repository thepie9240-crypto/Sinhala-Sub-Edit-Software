📖 How To Use — Sinhala Subtitle Editor

Watch the video, click a Sinhala subtitle to fix it, then save. Press F1 any time to open this guide.

🚀 Quick start
1
Load the videoClick 1. Load Video or simply drag & drop the video file onto the window. MKV / MP4 with any number of audio tracks is fine.
2
Load subtitles2. Load Eng SRT (optional, for reference) and 3. Load Sin SRT (the one you will edit). You can also drop .srt files — the app asks which language it is.
3
Watch & fixClick any Sinhala subtitle on the video (or press Tab) and type the correction. Enter saves it and continues playing.
4
SaveClick Save to Original SRT to overwrite the file, or Download New SRT to save a copy. Your work is also auto-saved every 5 seconds.
✨ Features

▶️ Player controls

  • Play / Pause: Space, the Play button, or double-click the video.
  • Seek bar: click anywhere on it to jump there, or drag it.
  • Skip: ← / → jump back / forward (5 s by default, changeable in Settings).
  • Volume: ↑ / ↓ or the mouse wheel over the video. Goes up to 200%.
  • Full Screen: the button; press Esc to leave.
In Full Screen the control bar hides itself. Move the mouse to the very bottom edge and it slides up (like VLC / PotPlayer).

🔊 Audio tracks

  • Every audio track inside the file is detected and listed in the dropdown next to the settings icon, with its language, codec and channels. ★ marks the default track.
  • Pick another track and it switches immediately — no waiting or loading, even for AC3 / DTS / E-AC3 audio.

✏️ Editing a subtitle on the video

  • Move the mouse over a Sinhala subtitle: it grows slightly and a "Click to edit" tag appears.
  • Click it (or press Tab) — the video pauses and an edit box opens. The box grows to show the whole text.
  • Enter or Tab saves and resumes playback. Shift+Enter adds a new line.
  • The English subtitle is shown at the top of the video for reference (position and size can be changed).
  • While the edit box is open: Ctrl+U puts {\an8} at the very start of the text (moves the subtitle to the top of the screen), and Ctrl+I wraps the whole text in <i> … </i> (italic). Press Ctrl+I again to remove it. Works in the list / window editors too.

🎨 ASS Tags mode

  • The ASS Tags switch next to Full Screen turns tag rendering on / off.
  • ON: tags such as {\an8}, {\pos(x,y)}, {\i1}, {\b1}, {\c&H00FFFF&} and <i>, <b>, <u>, <font color> are shown as styled / positioned subtitles instead of raw text.
  • A subtitle moved by a tag is edited by clicking it right where it appears; the edit box opens in the same place and shows the full text with its tags.
  • OFF: subtitles are shown plainly, tags included, as before.

📝 Subtitle List tab

  • Shows every subtitle with start / end time, duration, the English text (when loaded) and the Sinhala text.
  • Click a Sinhala cell to edit it. The video jumps to that line and pauses. The box grows so long lines are fully visible.
  • Auto Scroll follows the video. Scrolling manually switches it off; click the button to turn it back on.

🗔 Context Editor Shift

  • Press Shift to show or hide a floating editor on top of the video: English context on the left, editable Sinhala on the right.
  • Drag the title bar to move it. Drag any edge or corner to resize it. Its size and position are remembered.
  • Has its own Auto Scroll, Save and Download buttons.

🖥️ External Window F2

  • Opens the same live-synced table in a separate window — perfect for a second monitor.
  • It follows the video, highlights the current line and lets you edit just like the other tables.

🔍 Find & Replace Ctrl

  • Type a word to see every Sinhala line that contains it, with the word highlighted.
  • Untick the lines you do not want to change, type the new word and click Replace Selected.

🌐 Translate =

  • Press = to translate the current English subtitle into Sinhala (needs an internet connection).
  • Hover over a single English word (the video pauses) or select some text first, and only that part is translated.
  • Press = again or click the video to close the popup.

⚙️ Settings

  • Click the gear icon beside Full Screen.
  • Eng position & size, Sinhala size (default 50) and Sinhala colour (white by default, or pick any colour).
  • Table font size for the list, Context Editor and External Window, and the arrow-key skip time.
  • Everything is remembered next time you open the app.

💾 Saving & auto-save

  • Save to Original SRT writes straight into the Sinhala file you loaded — no dialog.
  • Download New SRT lets you choose a new file name and place.
  • Your edits are auto-saved every 5 seconds. If the app closes unexpectedly, reopen it, load the video again and it continues from where you stopped.
  • Clear Auto-Saved Data wipes the saved session and starts fresh.

🧹 Clearing English subtitles

  • Once an English SRT is loaded, a red ✖ Clear Eng Subtitle button appears. Click it to remove the English subtitle (and its columns) everywhere.
  • Load a different English file any time with 2. Load Eng SRT.

💡 Handy tips

  • The video file name is shown small at the top of the window.
  • The window size and position are remembered. It also adapts when you make it small.
  • Subtitle timing is never changed — only the text you edit.
  • Overlapping subtitles are all shown and each can be edited separately.
